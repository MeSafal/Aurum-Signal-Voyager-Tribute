The downloaded cover is the NASA-hosted image; no Wikimedia replacement was
needed. The 1990 dot is only 453×614, so the 2020 processed version is used
full-screen and both are never shown together. Voyager frames are cropped into
circular textures in the scale views; they are not stretched.

Mercury, Venus, Mars and the Sun have no verified Voyager photo in the supplied
set, so they are drawn as vector discs with gradients. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus
and Neptune use the NASA frames above.
