## Ephemeris

- JPL Horizons API: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/api/horizons.api
- Target Voyager 1 (`-31`), centre geocentre (`500@399`), daily vector table
  2026-01-01 → 2032-12-31, kilometres and seconds, ecliptic J2000.
- Regenerated by `tool/fetch_ephemeris.py` into
  `assets/data/v1_distance.json`.
- 173.14 AU and the light-time ladder are derived:
  `AU = km / 149,597,870.7` and `minutes = AU × 8.3167`.
